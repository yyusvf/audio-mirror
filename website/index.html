Streaming without the echo.

Audio Mirror plays sound like any other program, so a recorder may pick it up too. What we measured:

Capturing a device

OBS "Desktop Audio" records one device. The mirror plays on a different one and never shows up.

Capturing all applications

Discord screen share with desktop audio catches the mirror as well. Audio arrives twice and voice chat echoes back.

  1. Capture a device or a specific app, not "all applications".
  2. Untick voice apps like Discord or TeamSpeak for the mirrored device.
